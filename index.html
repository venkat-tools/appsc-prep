Advanced Math Shortcuts (గణిత షార్ట్‌కట్స్)

Time & Distance (కాలము - దూరం) Trains

Convert km/h to m/s by multiplying with 5/18. Convert m/s to km/h by multiplying with 18/5.

Time to pass a pole = Length of Train / Speed of Train

Ratios & Shares (నిష్పత్తి - భాగస్వామ్యం) Partnership

If A invests C1 for time T1, and B invests C2 for time T2, then their profit share ratio is:

P1 : P2 = (C1 * T1) : (C2 * T2)

Profit and Loss (లాభ నష్టాలు) Successive

If price increases by x% and then decreases by y%, the net change percentage is:

Net Change = x - y - xy/100

Clocks (గడియారాలు) Angles

Angle between hour hand and minute hand at H hours and M minutes is:

Angle = | 30H - 11M/2 | deg

High-Yield AP Welfare Schemes (ముఖ్యమైన పథకాలు)

Talliki Vandanam (తల్లికి వందనం)

Financial Assistance of ₹15,000 per year directly to mothers of school-going children (Class 1 to 12) from BPL families.

Annadatha Sukhibhava (అన్నదాత సుఖీభవ)

Financial aid of ₹20,000 per year to farmers (inclusive of PM-KISAN ₹6,000) to support input costs and agriculture purchase.

Adapaduchu Nidhi (ఆడబిడ్డ నిధి)

Monthly financial pension of ₹1,500 directly credited to eligible women in AP aged between 18 and 59 years.

Deepam Scheme (దీపం పథకం - ఉచిత సిలిండర్లు)

Provision of 3 domestic LPG gas cylinders completely free of cost every year to domestic gas connection holders.

Uchitha RTC Prayanam ( RTC బస్సులలో ఉచిత ప్రయాణం)

Free travel facility for all resident women in APSRTC Palle Velugu and Express buses across the state.

Nirudyoga Vruthi (యువగళం నిరుద్యోగ భృతి)

Monthly unemployment allowance of ₹3,000 for eligible qualified unemployed youth in AP until they secure employment.

Mental Ability Shortcut Formulas

Time & Work (కాలము - పని)

If A completes a work in \(x\) days and B in \(y\) days, then working together they finish in:

\(\text{Total Days} = \frac{x \cdot y}{x + y}\text{ days}\)

Average Speed (సగటు వేగం)

If a person travels equal distances at speed \(u\) and \(v\), the average speed is:

\(\text{Average Speed} = \frac{2uv}{u + v}\)

Compound Interest (చక్రవడ్డీ)

Formula for Amount \(A\) accumulated on Principal \(P\) at rate \(r\%\) for \(n\) years:

\(A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n\)

Reasoning: Coding-Decoding (కోడింగ్)

Always recall alphabetical positions using **EJOTY** mapping:

\(E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25\)

Preparation Strategy Tips

1. Understand the Weightage & Syllabus

In Group 2 Prelims, all 5 sections (History, Geography, Society, Arithmetic, Current Affairs) carry exactly 30 marks. Allocate equal time to all sections and avoid skipping any.

2. Manage Negative Marking (1/3rd)

APPSC has 1/3rd negative marking. While solving previous papers or mock tests, practice selective guessing. Only attempt questions where you can eliminate 2 options.

3. Revision is Key

Set aside the final 2 hours of every day to revise what you studied. Make short notes specifically for complex historical dates, articles of the Constitution, and AP bifurcation acts.
